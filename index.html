      How Raffler works

      • Add contestants with a name and a number of tickets. More tickets means better odds.
      • Bulk import by pasting Name, tickets per line or by uploading a CSV.
      • Pick winners — choose how many to draw, and whether each winner is removed from the pool.
      • Everything stays local. Your list lives in your browser — nothing is uploaded.
